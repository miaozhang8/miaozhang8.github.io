Miao Zhang

Postdoctoral Researcher
School of Computing Science, Simon Fraser University
email scholar github
I am a postdoctoral researcher at Simon Fraser University, working with Prof. Jiangchuan Liu. I received my Ph.D. in Computing Science from SFU, M.Eng. from Tsinghua University (advised by Prof. Zhi Wang), and B.Eng. from Sichuan University.

My research interests lie in algorithm-system co-design for deploying multimodal intelligence in resource-constrained, real-world environments, spanning cloud and edge computing, multimedia systems, and video analytics.
